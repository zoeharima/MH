About

Maia Harima is an Argentine-Japanese fashion designer and textile researcher based in Japan. Her practice focuses on material research, textile techniques, and transformation systems that extend the life of existing resources. Influenced by her Nikkei heritage, she approaches design as a way to reinterpret textile heritage, preserve traditional craftsmanship, and build new narratives where tradition, innovation, and sustainability naturally converge.
